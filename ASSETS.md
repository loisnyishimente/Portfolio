Square 1:1 high-end minimalist editorial 3D still-life for Lois Nyishimente’s developer portfolio. A single sculptural folded continuous ribbon forming an open interlocking abstract loop, reminiscent of elegantly connected systems. Tactile matte sage/olive green ceramic or rubber with one darker forest-green inner face, standing gently on a uniform warm ivory seamless studio background #f4f3eb. Sophisticated soft daylight from upper left, soft realistic ground shadow, physically plausible, substantial but airy form with pleasing negative space. Object centered, occupies about 76% of canvas, refined designer portfolio aesthetic, straight-on slightly elevated camera. No people, letters, typography, interface, logos, background gradients, frames, or watermark. Abstract artwork, not a product screenshot.
